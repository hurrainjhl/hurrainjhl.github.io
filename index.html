About Me

I'm a BS Cybersecurity student at Air University, Pakistan, currently in my final year. My passion is securing systems, detecting threats, and building open-source security tools that make a difference.

I've completed 5 internships across Red Team operations, SIEM engineering, DLP policy management, digital forensics AI research, and security audits. I've built 10+ open-source cybersecurity tools focused on penetration testing, vulnerability assessment, and threat intelligence. My work has earned recognition at CyLab Academy and TryHackMe.

During my Cybersecurity Solutions Internship at Trillium Information Security Systems (TISS), I mastered networking essentials (OSI, TCP/IP, routing, switching), completed CompTIA Security+ training, and specialized in Forcepoint Data Loss Prevention (DLP) — policy management, discovery tasks, incident response, and threat intelligence using AlienVault OTX.

At DEVLOGIX (Aug-Nov 2026, 3 months), I focused on security fundamentals, threat analysis, vulnerability assessment, and secure system design through real security challenges, audits, and protective measures. At NCSA (Jul-Aug 2026, onsite), I worked on digital forensics AI research and datasets.

My earlier internships include Red Team Intern at ISecFort (penetration testing, exploit chains, phishing simulations), SIEM Engineer at TechnoSec (log analysis, security monitoring, incident response), and a Web Intern at Webxperts International (secure web development with focus on secure coding and OWASP principles).

Outside work, I compete in CTFs, contribute to open-source security projects, write security research articles, and stay current with emerging threats and attack vectors.
